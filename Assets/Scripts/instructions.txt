triangle controller.cs 
Create 3 spheres in your scene: PointA, PointB, PointC

Attach this script to an empty GameObject (TriangleController)

Drag the spheres into the script's public fields (pointA, pointB, pointC)

Run the scene in Play Mode

Press spacebar → it logs the angles to a CSV file in:

Application.persistentDataPath (on your PC, typically a temp folder)

Create your TriangleController GameObject

Assign:

PointA, PointB, PointC = 3 spheres in the scene

xrCamera = the Main Camera (child of XR Origin)

Press Play with your headset on — the triangle will appear at your eye level and 2m in front

Customize Later
Use pointC as the adjustable sphere via VR controller

Add grab/translate input using the XR Interaction Toolkit

Lock movement to a plane (e.g., constant eye-height)
